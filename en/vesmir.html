FOREX IS AN OCEAN.

Forex is an ocean, not a pool. It's not about knowing everything, but knowing how to filter what's important and what's noise.

For the trader:The biggest market in the world will offer you movement every minute. A professional picks little from it and lets the rest float.

02 — Planet

COMMODITIES ARE THE BOTTOM.

Commodities are the bottom of the ocean. Where it's quiet, where the base is, where strength hides.

For the trader:Price is decided by what really exists: supply, demand, stock. Under the chart there is always a physical world.

Sector 03 · Time
03 — Planet

INDICES ARE SHIPS.

An index is a game with time. Time is a teammate, not an enemy. Indices are ships that sail on the ocean and carry you when you have patience.

For the trader:An index is an average of many companies, so it moves calmer than a single stock. The reward for patience is time.

Sector 04 · Vision
04 — Planet

STOCKS ARE A ROCKET.

Stocks are about believing in visions, not in numbers. A rocket in the sky, flying where no one has been yet.

For the trader:Vision without a plan for loss is just hope. A rocket can fall too, so the size of the risk is decided before launch.
